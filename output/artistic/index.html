Dazeland [jump here] [permalink] [Comments]
Praise for Dazeland, "the eldorado of old games". Anyone who has sought honest opinions on video games will have learnt to quickly distrust the gaming press; the incentives are too misaligned for them to give an honest appraisal.

Hence we turn to independent voices, and Dazeland is one such voice. The website is a trove of knowledge on old Amiga and PC games, with concise reviews provided for each one, carefully weighted scores, and a contrast with contemporary magazine reviews.

The website originates from France but is also written in English, giving outsiders a view into the lively French software scene of the recent past, with the continent's much-beloved Amiga well represented. I've long admired the rich bitmap graphics of that platform, and I'm pleased to say that Dazeland has plenty of screenshots, which are at least pretty to view even if the gameplay was mauvais!

Other notable features include a section filled with information on every MegaMan game, lists of recommended reading and curated links, and a jukebox - celebrating the other great delight of the Amiga with inspired pieces.

The website is still updated as of July 2025, and looks to have been online since the year 2000. Such dedication coupled with the high effort presentation earns it this place among the artistic sites. Vive la Daze!
Heaviness: Light Eccentricity: 9/10

Spectrum Crap Games Competition [jump here] [permalink] [Comments]
The ZX Spectrum 48k was the machine that made the British computing scene. Its humble hackish origins were what introduced a generation of programmers to their craft. It also served to entertain many growing up in the 1980s with its vast and varied selection of games.

This computer was long discontinued by the time I was born, but I admire its simplicity and what it symbolises. Modern systems are inscrutable; they hide their workings from the user. They aren't nearly as approachable to program.

In this spirit, there is held a 'crap games contest' every year where a group of Spectrum enthusiasts attempt to create the worst game they can for the system. The entries may be browsed on this site - itself taking heavy inspiration from the Spectrum's graphics palette.
Heaviness: Now Loading... CSSCGC Eccentricity: 9/10
