Light From A Dead Star [permalink]
The musical fansite is an old genre, and still it lingers in an underground of its own. Light From A Dead Star is such a site dedicated to Lush, the British shoegaze pioneers. The website hosts a trove of resources - hundreds of interviews and thousands of photos - compiled from magazines and press releases during the band's heyday in the early to mid 90s.

Pre-social media style, there is also a guestbook and a page where fans, friends and family have contributed their memories of the band.

This site is an effort beyond those 'brochure' style thrown up by record labels, and the same amount of passion and attention to detail heard from their music has been put into this website. On that matter, the sound of Lush is dreamlike. I recommend listening to their music now if you haven't already, with the widely available compilation "Gala" being a good place to start streaming.
