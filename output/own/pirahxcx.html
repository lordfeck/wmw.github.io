One aspect of the internet I miss is that it used to feel like a refuge from the crowd, from those who entertain thoughts of running against it, perhaps more so than from it. This site feels like stepping back into those days. It's created by someone who found the same freedom online. May the fire never go out.

Indeed, to that end pirah seeks to fan the flame. This he does by means of original thoughts, exquisite film recommendations, research into other free spaces online, and creating and building his own. But the author has just as colourful a life offline, living the type of life that has embodied as much antinomianism as it's possible to grasp in this stifling world. I also like the design.
