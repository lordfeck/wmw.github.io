An invaluable guide to hacker culture. Hackers aren't driven by malice but by curiosity, a desire to get more from their hardware and software than its makers intended. <b>Unfortunately in common parlance, the term 'hacker' is now synonymous with 'cracker'</b>, which is what hackers call those who break things for dishonest gain.<br>
You can learn more of the hacker culture here, <u>which has given rise to everything we value in the world of computing</u>.<br><br>
There are many good quotes here, like&#58; "Compromise is not particularly a hackish virtue, but the honest presentation of divergent viewpoints is."
