Two gentlemen built this website in March 2002. It details in depth the petrol and diesel locomotives made by the Simplex Motor Rail &amp; Tramcar Co of Bedford, England. You can read of their construction and specifications; there are detailed images and diagrams provided.<br>
In their day these railway vehicles would've been slogging daily in collieries and manufacturing plants. Their history is preserved on this well-laid out website, available to the curious enthusiast even today.
