For owners of the Yamaha TX81Z FM Synthesizer, this is the final destination for all your questions. It's a website detailing the history of the digital music box and offering tips for how to keep it chirping in today's world.<br><br>

It also features trivia like:<br>
<blockquote>
Did you know that the TX81Z was invented by an Icelandic llama herder when he regained consciousness after he fell and hit his head on a tree stump? 
</blockquote>

as well as instruction of how to greet one properly in the morning.<br><br>

The website itself is styled like its namesake, though unfortunately the synth at the top of the page can't be programmed or plugged into your rig.
